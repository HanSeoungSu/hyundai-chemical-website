01 / OVERVIEW

Two resin types,
one mixed bed

Samyang TRILITE SM210 combines H-form strong acid cation resin and OH-form strong base anion resin. It exchanges cations and anions to produce pure water.

It is used in deionizing cartridges without regeneration equipment. Suitability and replacement intervals depend on feed water, target water quality, flow rate and equipment conditions.

General marine use example

Mixed-bed ion-exchange resins are used in some onboard water-treatment systems that require purified or deionized water. This does not mean SM210 is automatically suitable for a particular vessel. Check the feed-water properties, required water quality, system configuration and the manufacturer's recommended model.
