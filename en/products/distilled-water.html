Basic substance information

Distilled water is water that has undergone distillation. Its CAS number identifies water, but does not certify a particular manufacturer’s purity or test results.

Duksan’s official catalog lists 4L Water with CAS No. 7732-18-5. This public listing alone does not establish the product number or grade of our catalog entry.

The CAS number identifies the substance. We confirm the actual manufacturer product’s grade, purity, packaging and MSDS individually.
