HISTORY

Our History

Since our establishment in 2012, we have continued to expand our supply capacity and safe storage facilities.

  1. 2012.07
    Hyundai Chemical established

    Nine outdoor hazardous-material storage tanks, one indoor storage building and one manufacturing building

  2. 2015.10
    Registered as a port supplier

    Registered for goods supply under the Harbor Transport Business Act

  3. 2015.12
    Outdoor storage expanded

    One additional outdoor hazardous-material storage facility

  4. 2017.11

    Recognized as an excellent workplace for risk assessment and as a CLEAN workplace
